What we do

Built to make
your work easier.

Orviton is a software company founded by Sabbir Ahmed. We build tools that take care of the problems slowing businesses down, in a way anyone can understand and use from day one.

We fund Orviton ourselves, so we answer to our customers and build only what is genuinely useful.

How we think
01
Start with a real problem

Every product begins with a problem we have seen businesses struggle with. We build to solve it, not to add features.

02
Launch early, improve often

We release early and learn from real customers. A working product that people use teaches us more than a perfect plan.

03
Keep it easy

If a tool needs a manual, we are not done yet. Everything we make should feel clear from the first minute.

04
Build for the long term

Reliable systems and fair pricing. We are not chasing a trend. We want to be a company you can depend on for years.
