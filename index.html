What we do

Independent.
Uncompromising.

We build software that solves one problem better than anyone else. Not platforms. Not all-in-one suites. Every product we ship is scoped tightly, works reliably, and gets out of your way.

How we think
01
Identify real friction

Every product starts with a workflow that's painfully inefficient. We don't build features looking for problems, we find problems worth solving.

02
Ship early, refine constantly

Real products come from shipping fast and learning from actual users. We'd rather have something imperfect in production than perfect on paper.

03
Keep it simple

Complexity is a cost. Every feature we add has to earn its place. Good software feels obvious to use.

04
Build to last

Production-grade infrastructure, reliable uptime, sustainable pricing. We're not selling a trend, we're building businesses that endure.
